# Q9 spec: MTP speculative decoding on Qwen3.8 (decided at the Fable gate, 2026-09-15)

> Written after Q9 step 1 (roadmap row Q9; `3b1f54d`, the chunked-path tier
> gate and the `[OPTIME] placement` accounting; the record's Q9 entry). The
> *question* was decided by that probe: with the chunked prefill finally using
> the tier (87.77 % against 0.95 % before the fix), row-at-a-time → chunked
> cuts the CPU expert count **7 069 → 5 016 (−29.04 %)** on Qwen3.8, where GLM
> measured **flat** (35 252 at every K from 1 to 16, record §SPEC-PROBE). The
> one property this whole family depends on — do adjacent tokens share the
> *cold* experts — differs between the two engines, so §SPEC-PROBE's ~1.2×
> refusal does not transfer and the row's own rule says: a spec. This is it.
> Evidence is §SPEC-PROBE, §Q-PROFILE, §Q7 steps 2–3, §Q10, §QP (d), the Q9
> row, and the checkpoint's `config.json` and safetensors index as read on
> the rig today; nothing below is inferred from anything else.

## Decision

**Build it, in the order below, with two kill gates in front of the build and
the build gated on a number those two gates produce — not on the "accepted
length ≥ 3.5" the row was written with.** That bar was set before anyone knew
what a verify-of-K forward costs on this engine; the gate this spec uses is
the predicted speedup itself, computed from the measured verify cost and the
measured acceptance, and the item is built only when it predicts **≥ 1.30×**
on the serving regime's warm-identical ms/token. Below that, the drafting
overhead and the rewind machinery eat the gain on a box whose run-to-run
spread is 3–5 %, and the item is not worth 5–8 days.

Four judgement calls this spec had to make, each with its arithmetic below:

1. **The dense stream decides whether a verify block is cheap, and today's
   engine only takes it to the GPU at S = 1.** `q38vk_dense_multi` refuses
   S ≠ 1 (Q7 left prefill on the CPU by design: "TTFT is out of this item's
   scope"). A verify-of-K forward is an S = K+1 call and would therefore run
   the whole 6.8 GB dense set on the CPU: ~90 ms per block at 75.7 GB/s,
   against 28.5 ms/token on dev0 today. That alone would put verify-of-2 at
   ~180 ms against 2 × 119, i.e. **no gain at any acceptance**. So the
   S-row GPU dense path is not an optimisation of this item; it is the item's
   precondition, and step 0 measures around it (below) while step 2 builds it
   first.
2. **The drafter is a whole extra layer, not a head.** The checkpoint's
   `mtp.*` is one hybrid layer — a full QSA attention block with its own
   indexer, plus a MoE with **512 routed FP8 experts** (top-10), a shared
   expert, both hyper-connection mixers, and two 2 560 × 2 560 input
   projections (`fc_embedding`, `fc_hidden`) — **2.51 GiB in total, 2.4 GiB
   of it routed experts**. Every draft token is one pass through that layer
   *plus one LM-head pass* (1.27 GB) to read the draft's argmax. The head is
   already on dev0 (2.6 ms); the layer's dense part (~190 MB) is ~2.5 ms on
   the CPU; its ten routed experts are cold to the tier and come from the
   page-cache mapping at ~0.17 ms each. **~7–8 ms per draft**, ~22–25 ms for
   K = 3, plus the catch-up pass (below). Priced, not ignored: it is 15–20 %
   of the block.
3. **Rewind is by replay, not by copy.** DeltaNet's state is 48 × 128 × 128
   f32 per layer = **3.15 MB × 36 = 113 MB**; snapshotting it per block would
   cost ~6–11 ms at memcpy speed, on every block. Instead the recurrence runs
   *out of place* over the block (read `state_in`, write `state_out`; same
   traffic as today), the block's per-row recurrence inputs are kept (~50 KB
   per layer per row), and on a partial acceptance A < K the accepted A+1
   steps are replayed from `state_in` — **(A+1) × ~2 ms** (`dn-recur` after
   Q10 is 1.97 ms/token over all 36 layers) and no copy at all when the whole
   block is accepted. The 4-tap causal conv window (3 rows per layer) gets the
   same ping-pong; the QSA KV and the MTP's own KV are position-indexed and
   truncate by resetting the length; the pooled-key indexer cache must
   recompute its last partial pool after a truncation (the one place a
   truncation is not a length reset).
4. **The MTP's routed experts stay off the tier in v1.** The tier's heat
   ranking is per (layer, expert) over 48 layers; adding a 49th row changes
   the histogram format and every preload that reads it. At ~1.7 ms per
   draft from the page-cache mapping, tiering them is worth at most ~5 ms per
   block — a step for after the item has a measured number, not before.

## In numbers — what decides the build

Per block of K drafts the engine pays one verify forward of S = K+1 rows,
`V(K+1)`, plus the drafting `D(K)`, and gets `E[A+1]` tokens, where A is the
number of accepted drafts (the verify token itself is always one). With
`T1` the single-token cost on the same regime:

    predicted speedup  S(K) = E[A+1] · T1 / ( V(K+1) + D(K) )

Every term is measured by the steps below. The bracket from today's numbers,
so the reader knows what the steps are expected to find and can see a
surprise for what it is:

| bucket (per block, S = 4) | scales as | today S = 1 | expected S = 4 | why |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| `dn-proj` + `qsa-proj` + `lm-head` on dev0 | ~flat in S (bandwidth-bound; the S-row tile reads each weight once) | 28.5 | ~31 | Q7's own S = 1 shader is per-row independent; the head must now run for **all S rows**, not the last |
| `shared` + `router` (CPU, memory-bound GEMV → GEMM) | ~flat | 12.2 | ~14 | same weights for every row |
| GPU experts `vk-issue` + `vk-take` | once per layer per block + per-expert work with ~49 % dedup (the hot-expert dedup §SPEC-PROBE measured on GLM) | 18.6 | ~28 | 48 submits per block instead of per token |
| `cpu-experts` | linear × (1 − dedup); dedup 29 % at 32 rows, **unknown at 4** | 9.6 | ~31 | the term step 0 exists to pin down |
| `gr-read` | per row, but rows are independent and the ~10 ms scalar part is single-threaded, so rows spread over 8 threads | 26.1 | ~28–35 | |
| `dn-recur` + conv/norms | strictly linear (sequential over rows) | ~4 | ~16 | |
| `qsa-attn`, PLE, rest | linear at short context | ~5 | ~20 | |
| **V** | | **~119** | **~170–180** | **V(4)/V(1) ≈ 1.45–1.5** |

    D(3) ≈ 3 drafts × ~7.5 + catch-up over ~2.5 accepted rows ≈ 30 ms
    break-even E[A+1] at K = 3  = (175 + 30) / 119 ≈ 1.7 tokens per block
    at E[A+1] = 2.0 → 1.16×;  2.5 → 1.45×;  3.0 → 1.74×;  3.5 → 2.0×

So the item lives or dies on whether the one-layer drafter lands **≥ 1.1
accepted drafts per block** on real text with greedy decoding, and on whether
the linear buckets really are as small as the sub-timers say. Neither is
known; both are half a week to find out; the build is 5–8 days. Hence the
order.

## Ordered build, with the gate per step

Each step is one branch, one commit series, the numbers in the commit body,
and the row in the record. Tiers per the roadmap's table.

### Step 0 — the verify cost curve `V(S)` (Sonnet, 1 day of work, ~2 h of rig)

Qwen has no integer chunk knob (`Q38_PREFILL_BATCH` is a bool, and the chunk
is `Q38_PREFILL_BATCH_ROWS = 32`, a compile-time constant). Add
**`Q38_PREFILL_ROWS`** (runtime cap on `q38_moe_prefill_rows`' `requested`,
default 32, off = today's behaviour — bit-identical, the knob only shortens a
chunk) and run `q9_probe.sh`'s prompt at rows = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 with
`COLI_TIMERS=1`, both legs on the fixed tier gate, fully resident, three
repeats, interleaved. Report per rows: ms/token by bucket (the prefill timer
bank, which §Q-PROFILE separated from decode's), the CPU expert count and
the GPU expert calls (§SPEC-PROBE's table shape). Then form

    V*(S) = V_cpu(S) − dense_cpu(S) + dense_gpu(1) · (1 + 0.1·(S−1))

i.e. replace the measured CPU dense bucket with the GPU S = 1 figure and a 10 %
per-row allowance for the tile (the P4 tiled shaders measured the S-row cost
essentially flat on GLM; the allowance is there to be beaten, not relied on).
Step 2a replaces this projection with a measurement.

**Gate (a kill, not a pass):** if `V*(4) / V*(1) ≥ 2.4`, no one-layer drafter
can reach break-even (it would need E[A+1] ≥ 2.7 before any drafting cost)
and the item is dead here, with the table as the reason. Expected: 1.45–1.5.
Also required: the S = 1 row of the chunked path must reproduce
row-at-a-time's expert counts to the unit (it is the same computation), or
the knob is wrong.

### Step 1 — the drafter and its acceptance (Opus, 2–3 days)

Implement the MTP layer forward on the CPU behind **`Q38_MTP_DRAFT=K`**
(0 = off, default): load `mtp.*` (the trunk's loaders and the trunk's
kernels — QSA attention with its indexer, `q38_moe` with the trunk's expert
cache in a **separate bounded slot set** so drafting cannot evict the trunk's
learned cache, the hyper-connection mixers, the shared head on dev0); the
input recipe (`fc_embedding` on the next token's embedding, `fc_hidden` on the
trunk's final hidden, the two `pre_fc_norm_*`, how they combine) **taken from
the reference modelling code for `model_type: qwen4_exp_text`, cited by URL
and commit in the commit body**. If no reference is reachable, say so in the
commit body, derive the recipe from the tensor names and shapes, and treat
the K = 1 acceptance sanity bar below as the correctness check — a wrong
recipe reads as a useless drafter, and the two must not be confused.

Measure acceptance **teacher-forced, with no verify path and no rewind**:
during ordinary greedy decode, after each committed token t, run the drafter
recursively for K = 4 from the true hidden state of t and the true token
t+1, and compare draft k with the token the engine itself commits k steps
later. Report, per prompt, the conditional acceptance `p_k` (draft k correct
given drafts 1..k−1 were), and from it `E[A+1]` for K = 1, 2, 3, 4. Three
prompts × 256 generated tokens: `datapoint.py`'s rotating prompt 1, a real
Open WebUI-shaped chat turn, and a code prompt. Also report the drafter's
own cost per step (`mtp-*` sub-timers: dense, experts, head, glue) — that is
`D(K)`'s per-draft term, measured rather than the ~7.5 above.

**Gates:** knob off, the binary is bit-identical to the pristine on
`teacher_forcing`, `last_logits` and `rome_bench` (the drafter must not touch
the committed path at all — it only *reads* the hidden state). **Sanity bar:**
`p_1 ≥ 0.5` on the chat prompt; below that the recipe is suspect and the item
does not proceed to a verdict on acceptance until the recipe is shown right on
a fixture. **The build gate:** with step 0's `V*` and this step's `E[A+1]` and
per-draft cost, `max_K S(K) ≥ 1.30`. Report every K, including the ones that
fail; the curve is the result. Below 1.30 at every K the item is **dead on
measurement**, the drafter stays in the tree knob-off, and the row records
the acceptance curve so nobody re-runs it.

### Step 2 — the build (Opus, 3–5 days), only if step 1's gate is met

In this order, each sub-step gated before the next:

- **2a. The S-row GPU dense path.** `q38vk_dense_multi_ex` accepts `S ≤ 8`
  for the DeltaNet and QSA projections and the head (all S rows of logits
  for the head; prefill's `q38_tf_emit` path is unchanged and still CPU).
  Per-row independent, so the oracle is Q7's: per-row logits within 1e-5
  relL2 of the S = 1 path, argmax unmoved, `teacher_forcing` identical over
  the long prompt. Measure `dense_gpu(S)` for S = 1..8 — this replaces step
  0's projection, and if `dense_gpu(4) > 1.5 × dense_gpu(1)` the arithmetic
  is redone before 2b starts. (A by-product: prefill could use it too; that
  is a prefill-track item, not this one — note it, do not build it here.)
- **2b. Verify-of-K in `step()` and the commit/rewind.** Draft K tokens
  (step 1's drafter, from the committed hidden state), run `step()` over
  the K+1 rows, accept the longest prefix whose argmax matches the drafts
  (exact greedy verification, so the committed text is the greedy text up to
  reassociation), commit A+1 tokens. Out-of-place DeltaNet state and conv
  window with replay on partial acceptance (judgement call 3); QSA KV, the
  pooled-key indexer cache and the MTP's own KV truncated to the accepted
  length; the MTP catch-up pass over the accepted rows (so its KV covers
  every committed position) before the next draft. **Per-slot**: every
  `--kv-slots` session carries its own MTP KV and draft state.
- **2c. Serving.** `Q38_MTP_DRAFT` honoured from the serve loop; the `DONE …
  STAT` line gains `drafted=` / `accepted=` (append-only, like P9's
  `reused`); `openai_server.py` untouched.

**Oracle for 2b/2c, by name.** Knob off: bit-identical to the pristine
(`teacher_forcing`, `last_logits max_abs 0`, `rome_bench` within spread).
Knob on: greedy text **identical over 1 000 generated tokens** on the three
prompts, with any divergence explained as a near-tie — the verify forward is
the chunked path, which the Q9 oracle leg measured at `max_abs 3.672e-05`
against row-at-a-time, so a flip is possible only where the top-2 logit gap
is below that; report the gap at any divergence, and a divergence with a gap
above 1e-3 is a bug, not a tie. `tworeq.py` at 4 slots IDENTICAL (each slot's
draft state is its own). **Decode gate:** `rome_bench.sh qwen38-vk`
rotating and warm-identical, interleaved A,B,B,A, ≥ 1.30× on warm-identical
and both candidate rotating runs above both pristine runs; `gate_ab_verdict`
decides. Also report `accepted/drafted` and `E[A+1]` from the serving run and
compare with step 1's teacher-forced figure — a serving acceptance well below
the teacher-forced one means the rewind or the catch-up is wrong, because the
drafter's inputs are the same.

### Step 3 — optional, after a measured number exists

Tier the MTP experts (a 49th histogram row, or a dedicated small tier on
dev2/dev3 where the trunk's experts already leave headroom) and move the
MTP's dense parts onto dev0 (~0.15 GB, ~0.1 ms/token by §QP (d)). Worth at
most ~5 ms per block; only if 2c's number is within reach of a round figure
that matters.

## Environment and configuration

| knob | default | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Q38_PREFILL_ROWS` | 32 | step 0: cap on the chunk's rows; ≤ 32; bit-identical at every value |
| `Q38_MTP_DRAFT` | 0 | K drafts per block; 0 = the drafter is not even loaded |
| `Q38_MTP_ACCEPT_LOG` | 0 | step 1: teacher-forced acceptance measurement (drafts computed and compared, never committed) |
| `Q38_MTP_CACHE` | 64 | slots in the drafter's private expert cache |

Every run with the drafter on is a run with 2.5 GiB more of the checkpoint
in play: warm the `mtp.*` shards (37–124 hold them, 2.4 GiB of experts in
60 and 62) with the rest and assert residency as always; a cold drafter
reads its experts from NVMe and its per-draft cost is a disk number.

## Measurement hygiene, inherited

Everything in `MEASURING.md` and CLAUDE.md applies unchanged: Qwen and GLM do
not fit in RAM together, so warm one and assert with `fincore`; 8 physical
threads pinned; the rig lock before the gateway is stopped, and the gateway
restarted on every exit path; `gate_lib.sh` decides, not the reader; arms
interleaved; the headline is the rotating median from `rome_bench.sh`, the
warm-identical row is the upper bound and is the row the 1.30× gate reads.
Two caveats carried in from the Q9 row, so they are not rediscovered: the
29 % dedup carries a residual placement component (94.06 % vs 87.77 % on the
tier) and is a bracket, and any batched-forward number taken on a binary
older than `3b1f54d` is a placement comparison, not a chunk-size one.
