# G4 spec: the KDA path (decided from G3, 2026-09-04)

> **Executed 2026-09-04; see record §G4 for what happened.** The decision
> (CPU, not a shader) held and the three steps landed bit-identical, taking
> `[OPTIME] kda` 2.301 → 1.598 ms/call and the rotating median 1.69–1.71 →
> 1.84/1.83. But **two claims below were wrong** and are corrected in the
> record rather than silently edited here:
> - the expected floor (~0.35–0.45 ms/call, "78 → ~12 ms/token") ignored the
>   dense projections and `ko` around the recurrence, which are **1.05 ms of
>   GPU submit round trips per call** — the real floor for this approach.
>   Achieved 1.598 ms/call, 78 → 54 ms/token.
> - the ordering assumed head parallelism was the win. It was worth 2%; the
>   conv-channel loop (step 3 here) was worth 2.7× on `coli_kda_step`.
>
> The stop condition in the ROADMAP procedure is what caught both.

The roadmap's gate after G3 asks for one page: which KDA approach, and the
numerics oracle by name. Evidence is the G3 section of
`ROME-3x7900XTX-2026-09-04.md`; nothing below is inferred from anything else.

## Decision

**CPU. Parallelize the recurrence across its 64 independent heads, stop
recomputing a constant, stop shifting the conv window. No GPU shader.**

The three roadmap options were built on a description of KDA that the
profile does not support: its inner loops are already AVX2-vectorized (GCC
`-fopt-info-vec`), the "scalar loop over L=512" belongs to MLA, and at
2.30 ms/call against a 0.9 ms single-core DRAM floor (16 MB of state traffic
at 18.8 GB/s) it is not bandwidth-bound — it is single-threaded, on one of
eight cores, with ~57k libm transcendentals and 24,576 twelve-byte
`memmove`s per call. Those three things are what the fix removes.

## What changes

1. **`expf(alog[h])` once per head, not 8,192 times** (`kda_layer`,
   `c/glm53.c`). `alog` is a weight; hoist the call out of the `d` loop
   into the `h` loop (64 calls per token instead of 8,192). Same `expf` of
   the same input: the same bits. No struct change, no load-time work.
2. **Head parallelism in `coli_kda_step`** (`c/delta_attention.h`).
   `#pragma omp parallel for schedule(static)` over `head`, guarded
   `#ifdef _OPENMP`. Every buffer in that loop is disjoint per head except
   `memory`, one `v_dim` buffer shared by all heads: each thread must get
   its own slice (`scratch + 3*width + omp_get_thread_num()*v_dim`), and
   `coli_kda_scratch_floats()` grows by `omp_get_max_threads()*v_dim` so the
   caller's allocation follows. Nothing about one head's arithmetic, or its
   order, changes.
3. **Channel parallelism for the conv pass** (same file). The same pragma on
   the `for (channel < 3*width)` loop; each channel's window slice and
   output are disjoint. The 12-byte per-channel `memmove` stays — spread
   eight ways it stops mattering.

*Not* a ring index for the conv window, which an earlier revision of this
spec proposed: `kda_window` is exported as a raw byte span for segment
migration (`ColiSegmentStateSpan` in `glm53.c`), and a position counter
would change what that span means. Step 3 recovers the same cost without
touching any state format.

That is phase 1, and it is the whole of G4. Expected: 2.30 → ~0.35–0.45
ms/call, **78 → ~12 ms/token**. Step-by-step procedure, commands and stop
conditions: `ROADMAP-2026-09.md` §"Executing G4".

Phase 2, *only* if phase 1 lands and the remaining ~41k transcendentals per
call (decay sigmoid, SiLU on the conv output, `expf(decay[k])`) still show
in `[OPTIME]`: a vectorized AVX2 `exp` approximation. That **changes
numerics** and ships behind an env knob, off by default, per CLAUDE.md.

## What does not change

The recurrence's arithmetic and its order within a head; `-ffast-math` is
not an option; no data leaves the CPU. `COLI_KDA_CPU` and the Vulkan
projection batching around the step are untouched.

## Oracle, by name

Phase 1 is **bit-identical**: `teacher_forcing` line identical *and* the
`--logits` `last_logits` line identical, checked with `diff` (not a
tolerance) against the pristine binary, 3 GPUs, the same histogram copy —
on the standard prompt *and* on a long one (there is no 690-token document
on the rig; build ~700 tokens by repeating `~/bench/prompt_glm.txt` thirty
times), so the recurrence and the conv window are exercised across many
tokens. The caveat in `README.md` applies: the resident set must be the same
in both runs, which the histogram copy guarantees.

`delta_attention.h` is included by `glm53.c` only — `kimi_k3.c` carries its
own `kda_forward`, `qwen36.c` nothing — so despite its docstring there is
no sibling behaviour to oracle. Both must still **rebuild clean**
(`make -C c kimi_k3 qwen36`), and the commit body says so; that is the
whole of what can be checked for them on this rig.

Phase 2's oracle is the record's: cosine, max-abs and argmax of the last
logits against pristine, shipped only within the documented near-tie
tolerance, knob off by default.

## Gate

`[OPTIME] kda` ms/call before and after in the same fresh-process run
shape (128 greedy tokens, `COLI_TIMERS=1`): **2.30 → ≤ 0.6**. Then the
serving number: `rome_bench.sh glm53` rotating median against G2's
1.69–1.71 tok/s, two runs, both reported. The `[OPTIME]` split is the
diagnosis; the rotating median is the result.

## Effort and tier

About one day, in three measured commits. **Sonnet**: every step is
specified to the line, the pattern (`#pragma omp parallel for` over
independent work) already exists in `ffn_layer`, and the header turns out
not to be shared. The one non-obvious requirement — the per-thread `memory`
scratch — is written out in the roadmap's procedure; a shared `memory[]`
across heads would corrupt every head's read-back while still producing
plausible tokens, exactly the failure the `README.md` says the
`teacher_forcing` line exists to catch, and the `diff` oracle is the guard.
Escalate to Opus only if a `diff` is non-empty or step 2 does not bring
`[OPTIME] kda` under 0.8 ms/call.

## Why not the shader, in numbers

A GPU recurrence kernel does not exist in `backend_vulkan.c`; it would be
from scratch. Its floor here: 16 MB of state over VRAM bandwidth ≈ 0.02 ms,
plus two ~55 µs submits, with 136 MB of state resident in VRAM ≈
0.15–0.2 ms/call → ~6 ms/token. Against the CPU plan's ~15 ms that is
~9 ms/token, 2.4% of the token, for 2–4 days and a second numerics path.
Revisit only after items 1–6 of the G3 ranked list are done, if KDA is
then the largest remaining bucket.
